Our beginnings

History of the Fraternity

The TAU GAMMA PHI was founded on October 4, 1968 at the University of the Philippines, Diliman, Quezon City. Six student leaders from the U.P. Political Science Club initially founded the Fraternity. Two were later expelled for violating fraternity rules, leaving the recognized Four Founding Fathers.

The early teaching framework was to establish a “protest” curriculum. The Founders offered a Fraternity to reform the highly violent campus fraternity culture by establishing an honor society rooted in anti-violence and the power of Reason.

A prayer for our fraternity and sorority

Prayer

Almighty God, bless this brotherhood and sisterhood of ours that we may succeed in all of its endeavour, enlighten and strengthen our Mindanao Grand Triskelion, Provincial Grand Triskelion and chapters Grand Triskelion, so for them to maintain the highest standards of decision making for a better and successful Tau Gamma Phi, Tau Gamma Sigma, Fraternity and Sorority, Amen.
